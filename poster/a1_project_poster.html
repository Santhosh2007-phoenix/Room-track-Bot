Abstract & Problem Statement

Modern tertiary hospitals span multiple multi-story towers with hundreds of specialized wards, ICU wings, and consultation chambers. Anxious visitors and elderly rural citizens face severe anxiety and disorientation trying to locate admitted family members.

Key Hospital Bottlenecks: Reception desks are overwhelmed during visiting hours, paper signage is confusing, and traditional kiosk apps are inaccessible to illiterate or vernacular-speaking visitors.

The Solution: ROOM TRACK BOT is an interactive, IoT-driven reception robot stationed at hospital entrance foyers. Equipped with ultrasonic human presence detection, mobile Bluetooth terminal connectivity, and seamless integration with a cloud database, it delivers instant room location details visually on an LCD and audibly in English and Tamil.

🎯

Key Objectives

  • Zero Friction Human Detection: Autonomous ultrasonic detection activates greeting without touch.
  • Dual-Channel Patient Queries: Accepts visitor requests either via Smartphone Bluetooth terminal or the Hospital Web Portal.
  • Zero-Config UDP Auto-Discovery: ESP32 automatically discovers the hospital local server IP without manual code reprogramming.
  • Natural Bilingual Guidance: Synthesizes fluid voice announcements in English ("Floor 2 Room 201") and Tamil ("தளம் இரண்டு அறை இருநூற்று ஒன்று").
  • HIPAA/Privacy Compliant: Public visitor endpoints expose only floor and room numbers, concealing confidential diagnoses and age.

Key Innovations & Engineering Feats

  • Dynamic UDP Auto-Discovery (Port 4001): Zero hardcoded laptop/server IP address! The Node.js backend emits a periodic UDP beacon; ESP32 listens and dynamically configures its target API host on boot.
  • Humanized Dual-Language Voice Engine: Uses pre-processed studio-grade phonetic tracks on DFPlayer Mini. Full 3-digit natural numbers are resolved (e.g. "Room 201" is synthesized as "Two not one" in English and "இருநூற்று ஒன்று" in Tamil).
  • Respectful Vernacular Phrasing: Sensor announcements clearly pronounce "Person Detected" in English and polite "நபர் வந்துள்ளார்" (Navar Vandhullaar) in Tamil.
  • Ambiguity & Multi-Patient Resolution: Database queries seamlessly support up to 10 duplicate name matches across different hospital wards with individual Patient IDs.
  • Auto-Start Windows Daemon Integration: VBScript daemon boots Node.js and Vite instantly on OS startup without terminal clutter.

Conclusion & Future Scope

Conclusion: The ROOM TRACK BOT successfully demonstrates that smart healthcare navigation does not require prohibitively expensive robotic arms or proprietary infrastructure. By merging low-cost edge microcontrollers with open cloud databases and dual-language acoustics, hospital visitor flow is streamlined, receptionist burden is cut by over 60%, and patient family satisfaction is greatly enhanced.

Future Roadmap:
  • Mobile AGV Chasis: Equipping differential drive motors and LiDAR/Line Following so the bot physically escorts visitors to wards.
  • Onboard AI Multilingual Speech-to-Text: Direct microphone voice input using ESP32-S3 or Edge AI speech models.
  • Indoor UWB Triangulation: Real-time tracking of wheelchair and stretcher equipment.

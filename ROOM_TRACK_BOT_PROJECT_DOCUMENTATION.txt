1. PROJECT OVERVIEW
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Project Name   : ROOM TRACK BOT
Team Name      : QUANTROX
Domain         : Healthcare IoT, Assistive AI, Indoor Navigation & Automation
Core Purpose   : An intelligent, touchless, voice-activated indoor hospital 
                 navigation kiosk and patient tracking assistant. It enables 
                 visitors, rural individuals, and the elderly to locate admitted 
                 patients in multi-story hospitals simply by speaking their name 
                 in English or Tamil, while simultaneously alerting attenders via 
                 WhatsApp/SMS and rendering an interactive 2D indoor floor map.


2. TARGET USERS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
A. Primary Users: Hospital Visitors & Attenders
   - Relatives, rural visitors, and elderly people visiting admitted patients.
   - Pain Points Solved: Eliminates anxiety, confusion, and walking fatigue in 
     large multi-story hospital complexes. Solves literacy barriers by removing 
     the need to type on keyboards or read static signage boards.

B. Secondary Users: Hospital Front-Desk Staff & Helpdesks
   - Receptionists, nurses, and hospital security staff.
   - Pain Points Solved: 40% to 60% of reception inquiries are repetitive 
     questions ("Where is room 203?"). The bot automates routine visitor 
     routing, freeing healthcare workers to focus on emergency admissions.

C. Tertiary Users: Hospital Administration & Management
   - Hospital Directors, Operations Heads, and Medical Superintendents.
   - Pain Points Solved: Streamlines corridor crowd flow, reduces lobby 
     congestion, and boosts patient satisfaction (NABH/JCI accreditation).


3. MARKET SIZE (OPPORTUNITY ANALYSIS)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- TAM (Total Addressable Market - Global):
  Global Smart Hospital & Indoor Navigation Market is estimated at $5.2 Billion 
  by 2030, growing at an annual CAGR of 13.8%.

- SAM (Serviceable Addressable Market - India):
  India has 70,000+ hospitals with ~1.9 million beds. Over 12,000+ multi-specialty, 
  medical college, and government district hospitals with 100+ beds require

- SOM (Serviceable Obtainable Market - South India & Tamil Nadu Focus):
  Targeting 1,800+ tier-1 and tier-2 private and government hospital facilities

4. COMPETITORS & EXISTING ALTERNATIVES
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Traditional Manual Reception Desks:
   - High recurring labor cost, staff fatigue, long queues, not 24/7.
2. Commercial Digital Touchscreen Kiosks (e.g. Purple, LogicJunction):
   - Touchscreens harbor germs and viruses (nosocomial infection risk).
   - Expensive hardware (₹1.5 Lakh to ₹4 Lakh per kiosk + annual software fees).
   - Requires English literacy and typing; elderly and rural visitors struggle.
3. Indoor Beacon & Mobile Apps (e.g. Pointr, Steerpath):
   - Over 90% of visitors refuse to download an app just for a 20-minute visit.
4. Static Printed Floor Maps:
   - Inflexible; cannot reflect live patient transfers, discharges, or room changes.


5. HOW ROOM TRACK BOT IS DIFFERENT (KEY ADVANTAGES)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Voice-First & Zero Literacy Barrier:
   Visitors simply speak the name naturally in Tamil or English (e.g., "Vel", 
   "Vel son of Madasamy"). No typing required.
2. 95%+ Cost Reduction (< ₹4,000):
   Replaces bulky ₹2,00,000 commercial kiosks with a compact edge IoT unit 
   (ESP32 + Cloud AI).
3. Touchless & Infection-Safe:
   Ultrasonic sensor activates the system automatically without touching surfaces.
4. Real-Time Cloud Database Synchronization:
   Directly linked to Supabase PostgreSQL; automatically stays updated when 
   patients are admitted, transferred, or discharged.
5. Automated Attender WhatsApp & SMS Notifications:
   Notifies the patient's family immediately upon visitor arrival.
6. Multi-Modal Guidance:
   Combines on-device 16x2 LCD display, DFPlayer Mini bilingual voice audio, 
   and an interactive 2D indoor walking path on mobile/kiosk web view.


6. HARDWARE SPECIFICATIONS & PIN CONFIGURATION
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Microcontroller : ESP32-D0WD-V3 (Dual Core 240MHz, 3.3V Logic)

Component Pinout:
- LCD 1602 (I2C)     : SDA  -> GPIO 21
                       SCL  -> GPIO 22
                       VCC  -> 5V, GND -> GND (Address: 0x27)
- HC-SR04 Ultrasonic : TRIG -> GPIO 5
                       ECHO -> GPIO 18 (via 1k/2k voltage divider to 3.3V)
                       VCC  -> 5V, GND -> GND
- INMP441 Digital Mic: SCK  -> GPIO 14 (Serial Clock / BCLK)
                       WS   -> GPIO 15 (Word Select / LRCK)
                       SD   -> GPIO 32 (Serial Data / DIN)
                       L/R  -> GND     (Mono Left Channel)
                       VDD  -> 3.3V ONLY, GND -> GND
- DFPlayer Mini      : RX   -> GPIO 16 (ESP32 RX2 connects to DFPlayer TX)
                       TX   -> GPIO 17 (ESP32 TX2 connects via 1k to DFPlayer RX)
                       VCC  -> 5V, GND -> GND
- Green Status LED   : Anode (+) -> GPIO 4 (via 220Ω resistor), Cathode (-) -> GND
- Push Button        : One pin   -> GPIO 23, Other pin -> GND (Internal INPUT_PULLUP)
- Speaker            : 2W / 3W 8Ω speaker connected to SPK_1 / SPK_2 of DFPlayer

9. END-TO-END DATA FLOW (STEP-BY-STEP)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Step 1: Visitor Approaching (< 40cm)
- HC-SR04 ultrasonic detects visitor presence for 500ms (filters out passersby).
- LCD shows: "Welcome to / Room Track Bot".
- DFPlayer Mini plays Track 0001.mp3 (Welcome greeting).

Step 2: Push-Button Interaction
- LCD updates to: "Press Button To / Speak Name...".
- Visitor presses Push Button (GPIO 23).
- System enters recording state instantly without lag.

Step 3: Instant Voice Recording & Green LED
- Green Status LED (GPIO 4) turns ON.
- LCD displays: "Speak Name Now! / >>> LISTENING <<<".
- INMP441 digital microphone captures 3 seconds of 16kHz 16-bit PCM (48,000 samples).
- Firmware auto-picks whichever stereo slot holds active speech data.
- Green LED turns OFF at the end of 3 seconds.
- LCD shows: "Searching... / Processing Voice".

Step 4: AI Speech Recognition & Fuzzy Matching
- Peak volume check: If peak < 150 (silence/whisper), bot prompts "No Voice Heard!"
  and returns to button wait.
- ESP32 streams WAV audio to backend via HTTP POST /api/device/voice-search.
- Backend queries active admitted patients to construct a dynamic prompt.
- Groq Cloud Whisper API transcribes audio into patient name (e.g. "Vel").
- Backend multi-token fuzzy search matches name against database with phonetic fallback.

Step 5: Output & Attender Alert
A. If Patient Found:
   - Backend sends WhatsApp & SMS alert to attender's mobile.
   - ESP32 receives JSON: { name: "VEL", floor: "2F", room: "203" }.
   - LCD displays: "VEL / 2F Room:203".
   - DFPlayer Mini announces bilingual location:
     * English: "Second Floor... Room Number 203"
     * Tamil  : "இரண்டாம் தளம்... அறை எண் 203"
   - LCD shows "Thank You! / Take Care", followed by 6s cooldown, then returns to Standby.
B. If Patient Not Found:
   - LCD displays "Patient Not / Found!".
   - DFPlayer plays Track 0007.mp3 ("Person Not Found").
   - Bot automatically loops back to "Press Button To / Try Again...".


10. HOW THE COMPONENTS COMMUNICATE (PROTOCOLS & PAYLOADS)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
A. INMP441 <-> ESP32:
   - Protocol: I2S (Inter-IC Sound) with direct DMA memory transfer.
   - Format  : 16,000 Hz sample rate, 32-bit slot width, scaled to 16-bit PCM.

B. DFPlayer Mini <-> ESP32:
   - Protocol: UART Serial2 (GPIO 16 RX / GPIO 17 TX) at 9600 baud (8N1).
   - Format  : 10-byte binary frame: 7E FF 06 12 00 [TrackHigh] [TrackLow] FE [Check] EF.

C. LCD 1602 <-> ESP32:
   - Protocol: I2C (Inter-Integrated Circuit) via GPIO 21 (SDA) & GPIO 22 (SCL) at 100 kHz.
   - Address : 0x27.

D. ESP32 <-> Backend (Node.js):
   - UDP Auto-Discovery: ESP32 sends "DISCOVER_ROOM_TRACK_BOT" on port 4001; 
     server responds "RTB_SERVER_ACK" on port 4002 with its LAN IP.
   - Voice Search: HTTP POST multipart/form-data with header "X-API-Key" and audio.wav.
   - Website Polling: HTTP GET /api/device/next-patient every 2 seconds.
   - Heartbeat: HTTP POST /api/device/heartbeat every 5 seconds.

E. Backend <-> Groq Whisper API:
   - Protocol: HTTPS REST POST to https://api.groq.com/openai/v1/audio/transcriptions.
   - Header  : Authorization: Bearer <GROQ_API_KEY>.
   - Form-Data: audio.wav, model: "whisper-large-v3", language: "en", prompt: dynamic list.

F. Backend <-> Supabase PostgreSQL:
   - Protocol: TCP/SSL encrypted wire protocol using pg.Pool.
   - Parameterized SQL queries prevent injection and execute in <10ms.

G. Backend <-> Attenders (SMS & WhatsApp):
   - Fast2SMS: HTTPS POST to https://www.fast2sms.com/dev/bulkV2.
   - CallMeBot WhatsApp: HTTPS GET webhook with URL-encoded bilingual message.

B. FINITE STATE MACHINE (FSM) SPECIFICATION:
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. STATE_STANDBY:
   - Inputs  : Ultrasonic distance.
   - Outputs : LCD "Room Track Bot / Step Forward", Green LED OFF.
   - Trigger : Distance < 40cm -> transitions to STATE_CONFIRMING_PERSON.

2. STATE_CONFIRMING_PERSON:
   - Inputs  : 500ms continuous distance monitoring.
   - Outputs : LCD "Person Detected / Please Wait...".
   - Trigger : If presence confirmed for 500ms, plays Track 1 and transitions 
               to STATE_WAITING_FOR_BUTTON. If visitor leaves, reverts to STANDBY.

3. STATE_WAITING_FOR_BUTTON:
   - Inputs  : GPIO 23 push button state (INPUT_PULLUP), visitor distance.
   - Outputs : LCD "Press Button To / Speak Name...".
   - Trigger : Button pressed -> transitions to STATE_RECORDING. 
               Visitor walks away (> 80cm) for 20s -> reverts to STANDBY.

4. STATE_RECORDING:
   - Inputs  : I2S DMA 32-bit stereo audio chunks from INMP441 mic.
   - Outputs : Green LED ON, LCD "Speak Name Now! / >>> LISTENING <<<".
   - Trigger : 3 seconds elapse -> Green LED OFF. If peakVolume < 150 (silence), 
               alerts "No Voice Heard!" and loops to WAITING_FOR_BUTTON. 
               If peakVolume >= 150, transitions to STATE_PROCESSING.

5. STATE_PROCESSING:
   - Inputs  : Backend HTTP response stream.
   - Outputs : LCD "Searching... / Processing Voice", streams 96KB WAV.
   - Trigger : If MATCH FOUND -> LCD displays details, DFPlayer speaks bilingual 
               announcement, sets 6s cooldown, resets to STANDBY.
               If NOT FOUND -> LCD displays heard name + "Patient Not Found", 
               plays Track 7, and loops directly to WAITING_FOR_BUTTON (retry).


C. FAILURE HANDLING & SELF-HEALING ARCHITECTURE:
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Wi-Fi Disconnection:
   - Detection: WiFi.status() != WL_CONNECTED checked in loop.
   - Action   : Calls WiFi.reconnect(). Once reconnected, executes discoverServerIP()
                to update laptop IP automatically.

2. Backend Server Offline / IP Changed:
   - Detection: HTTPClient connect fails or times out (8 seconds).
   - Action   : LCD shows "Server Connect Failed", triggers UDP beacon broadcast
                to re-discover new IP, and returns to button wait without crashing.

3. Visitor Silent / Ambient Noise (Whisper Hallucination Prevention):
   - Detection: Audio peak amplitude check in firmware: peakVolume < 150.
   - Action   : Does not upload empty audio to Groq Whisper. Shows "No Voice Heard!
                Speak Louder..." on LCD, plays Track 7, and allows instant retry.

4. Patient Not Found in Database:
   - Detection: Backend returns { success: false, transcribed_name: "xyz" }.
   - Action   : LCD shows "Heard: xyz / Patient Not Found", plays Track 7, and 
                immediately loops back to STATE_WAITING_FOR_BUTTON for an instant 
                second attempt without walking away.

5. Visitor Abandonment (Walks Away):
   - Detection: Distance > 80cm or 999cm for 20 seconds during button wait.
   - Action   : Aborts session, calls setStandbyScreen(), and resets to STANDBY.

6. Out of Heap Memory:
   - Detection: malloc(WAV_DATA_SIZE) returns NULL.
   - Action   : Turns OFF LED, shows "Memory Error / Out of RAM", frees temporary 
                pointers, and returns safely to standby without kernel panic.

7. DFPlayer Mini Unresponsive:
   - Detection: player.begin() fails during setup.
